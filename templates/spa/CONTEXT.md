# CONTEXT.md — your app's language

Words and ideas for **your** admin app. Your assistant keeps this updated when you plan features together.

## Template terms (buyer glossary — no implementation)

| Term | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Admin app** | The signed-in dashboard your team uses to manage your product. This SPA template is that app. |
| **SPA template** | A single-page app: one browser tab, fast navigation, no full page reloads between screens. |
| **Backend** | Your app's online brain (Express template). Sign-in, saved data, payments, and secrets live here. |
| **Client cache** | A quick-access copy of data the admin app already fetched, so screens feel instant when you go back. |
| **Live update** | When something changes on the backend, connected admin screens can refresh right away (via realtime). |
| **Practice mode** | The app works immediately with temporary sample data while you build — nothing is lost when it resets. |
| **Dashboard** | The main signed-in home after you log in — charts, tables, and controls for your team. |
| **Preview** | The admin app running on your computer (`pnpm dev`) before you put it online for your team. |

Add your product-specific terms below as you plan together.
