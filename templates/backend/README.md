# The part behind the scenes

Built with **VybeKiit**. This is the always-on piece that does the work behind the scenes: saving data, taking payments, sending emails, and letting people sign in. Your phone app and browser add-on use this piece so they stay simple and safe.

You do not need to read code to use it. Tell your AI helper what you want to build.

## Ready prompt for your AI coding tool

Open this project in [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview), [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/quickstart), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/docs), Grok, Kimi, Roo, [Zed](https://zed.dev/), Kiro, [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot), or any other AI coding tool, then paste:

```text
Set up the behind-the-scenes part.

I am a non-technical vibe coder. Read AGENTS.md and language.md.
Speak plain language. One action at a time. Translate any errors.
Celebrate progress. Do not dump technical steps on me.

Start with first-time setup until this always-on piece is ready for my phone app or browser add-on to use.
```

## Start here

Open this folder in your AI coding tool and say:

> **"Set up the behind-the-scenes part."**

Your helper takes it from there. It reads its instructions, walks you through the few steps only you can do (one at a time, in plain language), and gets this piece ready.

## Things you can ask for

- **"Set up the behind-the-scenes part"**: first-time setup.
- **"Let people sign in"**: accounts your phone app or browser add-on can use.
- **"Remember data"**: a place where the app saves information for real.
- **"Add payments"**: start taking money.
- **"Send emails"**: welcome notes, receipts, and sign-in codes.
- **"Something's broken"**: your helper runs a checkup and fixes it.

That's it. Everything technical is handled for you.
