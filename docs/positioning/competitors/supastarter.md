# Supastarter — Alternative & Comparison

> **Type:** Paid one-time · **Price:** $349–$1,499 · **Platforms:** Web only · **Last verified:** 2026-06-27
> Master data: [../comparison-matrix.md](../comparison-matrix.md) · Positioning: [../differentiation.md](../differentiation.md)

**Stack:** Next.js / Nuxt / TanStack Start, Better Auth, Prisma/Drizzle, PostgreSQL, Tailwind,
shadcn/ui, Hono, Trigger.dev. Tiers: Solo $349 / Startup $799 / Agency $1,499.

**What it is:** The most feature-complete paid SaaS boilerplate in the category — auth,
multi-tenancy, payments, admin, blog, i18n, and background jobs wired together for developers
building B2B web apps (1,400+ customers).

**Where it's strong:**
- Broadest payment coverage anywhere: Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem, Dodo Payments
- Full B2B multi-tenancy with organizations, seat-based billing, and RBAC out of the box
- Three framework variants (Next.js, Nuxt, TanStack Start) covering React and Vue
- Background jobs (Trigger.dev) and cron (QStash) — rare among one-time kits
- Explicit AI coding-agent support (AGENTS.md, Claude-ready, structured codebase)

**Where VybeKiit differs / wins:**
- **Audience gap:** Supastarter is built for developers who read diffs. VybeKiit's Decide+Guide layer
  targets the vibe coder who never wants to touch env vars or understand the plumbing.
- **Multi-platform in one bundle:** Supastarter is web-only; VybeKiit includes web + Expo mobile +
  WXT extension in a single purchase.
- **Live in session 1:** VybeKiit's onboarding ends with a live, paying app; Supastarter ships code.
- **Conflict-free updates:** maintained logic ships as `@vybekiit/*` npm version bumps, not git
  merges — meaningful for buyers who can't resolve conflicts.
- **Merchant of Record default:** Lemon Squeezy is VybeKiit's default (tax/VAT handled); Supastarter
  offers MoR-capable providers but leaves selection to the developer.
- **Price:** $29 vs $349 minimum.

**Honest — where Supastarter may be the better pick:**
- Senior/mid developers who want full control and to pick their own abstractions get more depth and
  framework choice.
- For complex B2B products needing **multi-tenancy + background jobs pre-built from day one**,
  Supastarter is more mature than anything VybeKiit ships today.
